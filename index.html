The problem

The answers exist. They're just spread across too many pages.

Working on the front line of a public dental service, I saw the same pattern every day. People had to piece together eligibility, costs, referrals and what to bring from separate web pages written in service language. Many gave up and called, often without the card or document they needed.

Getting there

The result: long phone queues filled with questions a short conversation could answer, and staff time spent re-explaining the same rules instead of helping people who need it most.

What DentMate does

A few questions, in the right order.

Instead of asking people to read policy, DentMate asks them about themselves. Each answer narrows the path, the same way an experienced staff member would screen a call.

  • Take it with you. Visit cards are designed to be saved or texted to yourself.
  • Knows its limits. Points people in pain to urgent care instead of guessing.
How I built it

From a frontline problem to a tested prototype.

  1. 1

    Started from real calls

    Mapped the questions people actually ask and the order staff answer them in, from nearly four years on the front line.

  2. 2

    Structured the knowledge

    Turned scattered information into one structured reference document so the assistant answers from a single source, not from guesswork.

  3. 3

    Designed the conversation

    Wrote the system prompt and a step-by-step screening flow, so the assistant asks before it answers.

  4. 4

    Tested it against itself

    Compared baseline and grounded versions of Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini on realistic patient scenarios, and logged every factual error.

  5. 5

    Moving it into code

    Now building a working prototype in GitHub with AI-assisted development, with privacy designed in from the start.

Designed for trust

Healthcare AI has to be careful by default.

Private by design

The prototype is being built so search runs locally and nothing a person types is sent to an outside service.

Grounded, not invented

DentMate answers from one checked reference document rather than a model's general knowledge, and testing focused on catching factual errors.

A guide, not a gatekeeper

DentMate prepares people for the call. Staff still make every decision about care.
